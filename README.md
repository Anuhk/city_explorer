# 🌍 City Explorer

### **Don't just visit a city. Explore it.**

Ever arrived in a new city and wondered, **“What should I explore next?”**

Whether you're a tourist visiting for the first time or you're on a short business trip with only a few hours to spare, discovering what a city has to offer can be surprisingly difficult. Most of us know the few famous landmarks, but there are often many more interesting places waiting to be explored.

**City Explorer** is a simple web application built to solve this problem.

Choose a city, check its upcoming **5-day weather forecast**, and discover **popular places worth exploring** — all in one place, without spending hours researching beforehand.

> **Less time searching. More time exploring. 🗺️**

---

## Problem

When visiting an unfamiliar city, travelers often face a simple question:

### *“Where should I go now?”*

You might have already visited the most famous attraction, but finding the next place to explore requires searching through multiple websites, blogs, videos, and reviews.

This becomes even more challenging when you:

* Are visiting a city for the first time
* Have limited time to explore
* Are travelling for a business trip
* Don't have time to research the city beforehand
* Don't know about places beyond the most famous attractions

**City Explorer brings this information together in one simple interface.**

---

## Our Solution

City Explorer allows users to select a city and quickly discover:

* **5-day weather forecast** to help plan the trip
* **Tourist destinations** worth exploring
* **Useful information** about each destination
* A quick introduction to the selected city

The goal is simple:

> **Help travelers make the most of the time they have in a new city.**
